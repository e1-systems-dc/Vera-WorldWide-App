# VERA WORLDWIDE: ECONOMY & GAMIFICATION MATRIX
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Purpose:** Defines the rules for in-app currencies, energy systems, streak mechanics, and subscription tier limitations.

---

## 1. CORE CURRENCIES & METRICS

### A. Sparks (The Soft Currency)
* **Purpose:** Used to purchase premium AI Personas, avatar customizations, and profile badges in the Vera Store.
* **Earning Matrix:**
  * Complete Standard Lesson: +50 Sparks
  * Perfect Lesson (100% Fluency Score): +20 Sparks Bonus
  * Clear a Boss Scenario: +250 Sparks
  * Daily Login Bonus: +10 Sparks (multiplied by current Flame streak, cap at 5x)
* **Tier Multipliers:** * "Curious" (Free): 1x Earn Rate
  * "Plus" & "Premium": 2x Earn Rate

### B. Zaps (The Energy/Stamina System)
* **Purpose:** Limits how many interactions/lessons a user can do per day to encourage retention and monetization.
* **Usage Cost:** * Start Standard Lesson: -1 Zap
  * Start Boss Scenario: -3 Zaps
  * Arcade Match: -2 Zaps
* **Regeneration Rules:**
  * Free/Plus users regenerate 1 Zap every 4 hours.
  * Daily reset occurs at 00:00 UTC (Fills to the user's tier max).

### C. Flames (The Streak System)
* **Purpose:** Tracks consecutive daily engagement.
* **Rules:**
  * User must complete at least 1 lesson or roleplay per day to increment the Flame count by +1.
  * Missing a day resets the active Flame count to 0.
  * "Streak Freeze" can be purchased in the Vera Store for 500 Sparks (Max 2 inventory limit).

## 2. SUBSCRIPTION TIER ENFORCEMENT

### TIER 0: "Curious" (Free)
* **Max Zaps (Cap):** 5 Zaps
* **Sparks Earn Rate:** 1.0x
* **Access Restrictions:** * Standard Lesson Path ONLY.
  * Boss Scenarios are LOCKED.
  * Arcade Mode is LOCKED.
  * Standard Vera AI Persona ONLY.

### TIER 1: "Plus" ($4.99/mo)
* **Max Zaps (Cap):** 15 Zaps
* **Sparks Earn Rate:** 2.0x
* **Access Restrictions:**
  * Unlocks Boss Scenarios.
  * Unlocks Fluency Mode (adaptive speed).
  * Arcade Mode is LOCKED.

### TIER 2: "Premium / Total Immersion" ($9.99/mo)
* **Max Zaps (Cap):** INFINITE (Zaps are hidden from the UI for this tier).
* **Sparks Earn Rate:** 2.0x
* **Access Restrictions:**
  * Unlocks Vocab Clash Arcade (Multiplayer).
  * Unlocks "Keep Fluency Sandbox" (Endless open-ended conversations).
  * Unlocks ALL Premium AI Personas for free.
